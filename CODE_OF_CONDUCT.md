# Code of Conduct

SPOOL is an engineering community. Participation is welcome regardless of experience level, background or employer, provided discussion remains technically rigorous and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people.
- Give reproducible technical reasoning when disagreeing.
- Assume good faith while still asking for proof where correctness or security matters.
- Make space for first-time contributors to ask precise questions.
- Respect privacy: never post private datasets, credentials, security-sensitive material or another person's personal information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending vendors, products or benchmarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, fabricated benchmarks, deceptive contribution activity and attempts to manipulate stars/votes/contributor counts are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being posted publicly.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Include links and enough context to investigate; do not escalate by reposting private or identifying information publicly.
